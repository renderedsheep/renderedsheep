|💰 **Commercialization Workbench** · [try it!](https://commercializationworkbench.com) | A free, private self-assessment that shows founders and researchers what could stop their technology from being adopted, what evidence they have, and what to test next. |🏎️ **[Seacrest Racer](https://github.com/renderedsheep/seacrest-racer)** | A Ridge Racer-inspired arcade racer for the Sega Genesis / Mega Drive, with a pseudo-3D coast road, three rivals and a real polygon player car drawn by the console's 68000 CPU. Includes a no-code SDK: a browser-based track and car editor that writes new cartridges without recompiling. Runs in emulators and on flash carts. C, 68000 assembly, Python, JavaScript. |
|🗺️ **[Chernobyl Zone Atlas](https://github.com/renderedsheep/chernobyl-atlas)** · [try it!](https://renderedsheep.github.io/chernobyl-atlas/) | An interactive map of the Chernobyl exclusion zone, drawn on eight Soviet General Staff military topographic maps aligned to modern coordinates. Covers the power plant, Pripyat, the Duga radar, waste sites and 208 settlements read by hand from the maps, with photos, the 1986 fallout traces and the Soviet kilometre grid. Visitors can suggest corrections. Companion to the SKALA Simulator. JavaScript, Python. |
|☢️ **[SKALA Simulator](https://github.com/renderedsheep/skala-simulator)** · [try it!](https://renderedsheep.github.io/skala-simulator/) | A browser reconstruction of SKALA, the process computer at Chernobyl Unit 4, running on the nuclear reactor's real control-rod map. Built from and checked against the IAEA's INSAG-7 report, Nikolai Karpan's book and the surviving recorder logs. JavaScript, Python. |
|✈️ **[R/C MiG-21 Flight Stabilizer](https://github.com/renderedsheep/mig21-flight-stabilizer)** | A DIY gyro stabilizer for a 1:10 scale R/C MiG-21 jet fitted with an electric ducted fan (EDF), built on an ESP32 and an MPU-6050 motion sensor. C++. VERY much a work in progress. |
|🎮 **[PlayStation Serial Utility](https://github.com/renderedsheep/PlayStation-Serial-Utility)** | Sends compiled homebrew from a PC directly into a PS1's memory over a cheap USB-to-serial cable, so code can be tested on real hardware without burning discs. PSXSERIAL-compatible. Python. |
|🌐 **[ESP Network Monitor](https://github.com/renderedsheep/ESP-Network-Monitor)** | An ESP32 panel that monitors up to nine devices or servers on a network, one LED each. Green means up and running; red indicates two missed checks. C++. |
